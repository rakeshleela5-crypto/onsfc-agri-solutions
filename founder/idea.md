# ONSFC Agri Solutions Private Limited
### Non-Perishable Agricultural Commodity Supply Chain

- **What it is:** A first-mile agricultural procurement, grading, and direct-supply enterprise operating out of a 1-acre aggregation yard and dry warehouse shed in Gooty Mandal, Ananthapuramu District, Andhra Pradesh.
- **Who it is for:** 
  - *Supply Side:* Over 5,000 dryland farmers across the 24 villages of Gooty Mandal cultivating groundnuts, pulses (toor/chana), millets, and cotton.
  - *Demand Side:* High-volume institutional buyers: peanut oil extraction mills, dal mills, snack/chikki manufacturers, residential schools and college hostels (Narayana, Sri Chaitanya, social welfare campuses), private hospital canteens, and supermarket dry-grocery private labels.
- **What it sells, at what price:** Graded, moisture-tested, and cleaned non-perishable agricultural commodities (Groundnut pods/kernels, Toor Dal, Bengal Gram, Millets, Cotton) packaged in standardized 50kg bags at wholesale rates benchmarked 8-12% below volatile mandi broker prices.
- **Where and how:** Hub at Gooty (strategic junction on NH-44 and major railway hub). Farm-gate pickup coordinated via Village Sarpanches, Gram Sachivalayams, and Rythu Bharosa Kendras (RBKs) through automated WhatsApp harvest scheduling. Direct delivery via freight trucks to buyers in Anantapur, Kurnool, Adoni, Guntakal, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad.
- **Budget and constraints:** 1-Acre yard and dry shed infrastructure; initial startup capex of ~₹5.65 Lakhs for shed setup, electronic weighing scales, moisture testers, and regulatory licenses; working capital financed via credit facility and Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) 3% interest subvention.
