## The idea

#### Non-Perishable Agricultural Commodity Supply Chain

- **What it is:** A first-mile agricultural procurement, grading, and direct-supply enterprise operating out of a 1-acre aggregation yard and dry warehouse shed in Gooty Mandal, Ananthapuramu District, Andhra Pradesh.
- **Who it is for:** 
  - *Supply Side:* Over 5,000 dryland farmers across the 24 villages of Gooty Mandal cultivating groundnuts, pulses (toor/chana), millets, and cotton.
  - *Demand Side:* High-volume institutional buyers: peanut oil extraction mills, dal mills, snack/chikki manufacturers, residential schools and college hostels (Narayana, Sri Chaitanya, social welfare campuses), private hospital canteens, and supermarket dry-grocery private labels.
- **What it sells, at what price:** Graded, moisture-tested, and cleaned non-perishable agricultural commodities (Groundnut pods/kernels, Toor Dal, Bengal Gram, Millets, Cotton) packaged in standardized 50kg bags at wholesale rates benchmarked 8-12% below volatile mandi broker prices.
- **Where and how:** Hub at Gooty (strategic junction on NH-44 and major railway hub). Farm-gate pickup coordinated via Village Sarpanches, Gram Sachivalayams, and Rythu Bharosa Kendras (RBKs) through automated WhatsApp harvest scheduling. Direct delivery via freight trucks to buyers in Anantapur, Kurnool, Adoni, Guntakal, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad.
- **Budget and constraints:** 1-Acre yard and dry shed infrastructure; initial startup capex of ~₹5.65 Lakhs for shed setup, electronic weighing scales, moisture testers, and regulatory licenses; working capital financed via credit facility and Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) 3% interest subvention.

### 2. Common Risks Raised by Multiple Board Members

1. **The Working Capital Trap (Cash Flow Mismatch):**
   * *Problem:* Farmers demand and deserve 24-hour bank transfer upon intake. If institutional buyers (hostels, oil mills, supermarkets) take 15 to 30 days to clear invoices, scaling to 60 quintals/day requires ₹4 Lakhs of cash rolling daily.
   * *Mitigation:* Secure a Cash Credit (CC) / Overdraft facility backed by the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) and enforce strict 7-day payment limits or 50% dispatch advances on private buyers.
2. **Moisture & Quality Disputes:**
   * *Problem:* Non-perishable crops (groundnuts, toor dal) can suffer internal mold or high moisture content (>9%) if harvested after unseasonal rains. If an oil mill rejects a 10-tonne truckload in Kurnool or Adoni, ONSFC bears freight and distress sale losses.
   * *Mitigation:* Portable digital moisture meter testing at the farmer's gate before loading onto the truck.
3. **Operational Clutter (Attempting Too Many Crops):**
   * *Problem:* Trying to aggregate 6 different crops immediately will overwhelm the Gooty shed team and create logistical chaos.
   * *Mitigation:* Focus 100% of volume on **Groundnut (Peanut)** and **Red Gram (Toor Dal)** for the first 90 days.

### 3. Mandatory Conditions Checklist for Launch

- [ ] **Condition 1 (Crop Focus):** Restrict launch operations exclusively to Groundnut and Red Gram in the first quarter.
- [ ] **Condition 2 (Territory Monopolization):** Focus exclusively on the 24 villages of Gooty Mandal until achieving 60 quintals/day steady volume before expanding into Guntakal or Tadipatri.
- [ ] **Condition 3 (Intake Transparency):** Install certified 2-Tonne digital platform scales with LED external display and keep a 50kg standard test weight visible for farmers to verify accuracy.
- [ ] **Condition 4 (Moisture Protocol):** Digital moisture testing (<8.5% for groundnuts, <10% for pulses) conducted and printed on the intake slip in the farmer's presence.
- [ ] **Condition 5 (Credit Guardrail):** Maximum credit to institutional buyers capped at 7 days. For new commercial buyers, require 50% advance before truck leaves the Gooty yard.

### 4. The Strongest Version of the Business

The strongest version of ONSFC Agri Solutions is not a generic agricultural trading firm, but **the undisputed First-Mile Procurement Monopoly of Gooty Mandal**. By anchoring your 1-acre yard directly on the NH-44 corridor, partnering with the 24 Village Sarpanches, and offering farmers the single magical experience of **transparent digital weighing + 0% commission + 24-hour bank transfer**, ONSFC becomes the trusted farm-gate institution that traditional middlemen cannot dislodge. You buy clean, non-perishable staples, bag them in standardized 50kg bags, and supply high-volume oil mills in Kurnool, dal mills in Guntakal, and residential campuses in Anantapur with guaranteed quality at lower-than-mandi costs.

## The offer

### ONSFC Agri Solutions Private Limited

---

#### PART 1: The Farmer Grand Slam Offer
**Offer Name:** *"రైతు బంధు స్పాట్ చెల్లింపు పథకం" (The Direct Farm-Gate Fair-Trade Guarantee)*

##### 1. The Problems Farmers Face with Middlemen:
1. *Mechanical Scale Tampering:* Middlemen fudge weights by 5kg to 10kg per quintal.
2. *Predatory Commissions:* Mandi agents deduct 4% to 8% commission (Dalali) from the farmer's payout.
3. *Delayed Payouts:* Farmers receive handwritten slips and wait weeks or months to get paid.
4. *Transport Burden:* Small farmers must arrange their own expensive auto-rickshaw/tractor to take 10 bags to the town mandi.

##### 2. The ONSFC Farmer Solution Stack:
* **The Core:** Farm-gate procurement of Groundnut & Toor Dal at APMC benchmark rates with **0% Commission Deduction** (saving farmers ₹300-₹500 per quintal).
* **Bonus 1 (Certified Digital Weighing):** High-precision digital scale with instant printed weight slip showing Gross, Tare, and Net Weight.
* **Bonus 2 (Free Standard Bags):** Clean, standardized 50kg HDPE bags provided free at the farm gate.
* **Bonus 3 (Direct Bank Transfer within 24 Hours):** 100% digital settlement via NEFT/RTGS/IMPS directly to the farmer's bank account or UPI—zero promissory notes.
* **The Guarantee:** *"If our digital scale does not match the official APMC test weight, we will pay you 10% extra on your entire harvest on the spot."*

---

#### PART 2: The Institutional Buyer Grand Slam Offer
**Offer Name:** *"The Reliable Harvest B2B Contract" (For Oil Mills, Dal Mills, Hostels & Supermarkets)*

##### 1. The Problems Institutional Buyers Face with Mandi Traders:
1. *Inconsistent Quality:* Bags filled with dust, stones, broken seeds, and high moisture.
2. *Erratic Supply:* Mandi supply swings wildly based on daily speculation.
3. *Price Gouging:* Middlemen mark up prices by 15% to 25% above farm-gate levels.
4. *No Single-Invoice Accountability:* Cash transactions with no GST invoices or lab moisture certificates.

##### 2. The ONSFC Buyer Solution Stack:
* **The Core:** Graded, moisture-tested non-perishable commodities delivered directly to the buyer's receiving bay at **8% to 12% below open mandi broker rates**.
* **Bonus 1 (Certified Lab Slip):** Every batch comes with a printed moisture level certificate (<8.5%) and foreign matter declaration (<1%).
* **Bonus 2 (Scheduled Morning Logistics):** Trucks arrive at the factory/canteen receiving bay on fixed pre-scheduled days with complete transit e-Way bills.
* **Bonus 3 (Single-Point GST Invoicing):** 100% compliant tax documentation for seamless accounting.
* **The Guarantee:** *"100% Quality Acceptance Guarantee: If any bag contains foreign matter exceeding 1.5% or moisture exceeding 9%, ONSFC issues an immediate credit note or replaces the bag within 24 hours at our cost."*
