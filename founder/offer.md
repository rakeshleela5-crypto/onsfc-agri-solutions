# The Irresistible Offer Architecture (/founder-offer)
## ONSFC Agri Solutions Private Limited

---

### PART 1: The Farmer Grand Slam Offer
**Offer Name:** *"రైతు బంధు స్పాట్ చెల్లింపు పథకం" (The Direct Farm-Gate Fair-Trade Guarantee)*

#### 1. The Problems Farmers Face with Middlemen:
1. *Mechanical Scale Tampering:* Middlemen fudge weights by 5kg to 10kg per quintal.
2. *Predatory Commissions:* Mandi agents deduct 4% to 8% commission (Dalali) from the farmer's payout.
3. *Delayed Payouts:* Farmers receive handwritten slips and wait weeks or months to get paid.
4. *Transport Burden:* Small farmers must arrange their own expensive auto-rickshaw/tractor to take 10 bags to the town mandi.

#### 2. The ONSFC Farmer Solution Stack:
* **The Core:** Farm-gate procurement of Groundnut & Toor Dal at APMC benchmark rates with **0% Commission Deduction** (saving farmers ₹300-₹500 per quintal).
* **Bonus 1 (Certified Digital Weighing):** High-precision digital scale with instant printed weight slip showing Gross, Tare, and Net Weight.
* **Bonus 2 (Free Standard Bags):** Clean, standardized 50kg HDPE bags provided free at the farm gate.
* **Bonus 3 (Direct Bank Transfer within 24 Hours):** 100% digital settlement via NEFT/RTGS/IMPS directly to the farmer's bank account or UPI—zero promissory notes.
* **The Guarantee:** *"If our digital scale does not match the official APMC test weight, we will pay you 10% extra on your entire harvest on the spot."*

### PART 2: The Institutional Buyer Grand Slam Offer
**Offer Name:** *"The Reliable Harvest B2B Contract" (For Oil Mills, Dal Mills, Hostels & Supermarkets)*

#### 1. The Problems Institutional Buyers Face with Mandi Traders:
1. *Inconsistent Quality:* Bags filled with dust, stones, broken seeds, and high moisture.
2. *Erratic Supply:* Mandi supply swings wildly based on daily speculation.
3. *Price Gouging:* Middlemen mark up prices by 15% to 25% above farm-gate levels.
4. *No Single-Invoice Accountability:* Cash transactions with no GST invoices or lab moisture certificates.

#### 2. The ONSFC Buyer Solution Stack:
* **The Core:** Graded, moisture-tested non-perishable commodities delivered directly to the buyer's receiving bay at **8% to 12% below open mandi broker rates**.
* **Bonus 1 (Certified Lab Slip):** Every batch comes with a printed moisture level certificate (<8.5%) and foreign matter declaration (<1%).
* **Bonus 2 (Scheduled Morning Logistics):** Trucks arrive at the factory/canteen receiving bay on fixed pre-scheduled days with complete transit e-Way bills.
* **Bonus 3 (Single-Point GST Invoicing):** 100% compliant tax documentation for seamless accounting.
* **The Guarantee:** *"100% Quality Acceptance Guarantee: If any bag contains foreign matter exceeding 1.5% or moisture exceeding 9%, ONSFC issues an immediate credit note or replaces the bag within 24 hours at our cost."*
