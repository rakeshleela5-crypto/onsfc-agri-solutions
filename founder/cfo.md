Every number below comes from the input file. Nothing is looked up or guessed.

## The margin that matters

Fixed costs: $92,000 a month (1-Acre yard lease & dry godown shed $25,000, Warehouse supervisor & 3 sorting/loading staff $55,000, Power, weighing scale calibration & godown maintenance $8,000, Admin, software, WhatsApp gateway & mobile communications $4,000).

- **Break-even: 9 quintals a day.** Below that you lose money every month.
- **Profit margin at your plan** (60 a day): **5%** of every sale, after every cost.
- Capacity: 150 a day.
