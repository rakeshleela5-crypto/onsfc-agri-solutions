# The Product Lens (Steve Jobs Framework)
**Score: 9.0 / 10 | Vote: FUND**

## What is Strong
1. **Ruthless Focus (No Fruits / No Vegetables):** Stripping away perishable tomatoes and bananas is brilliant product discipline. Grains, groundnut, and pulses can sit safely in the Gooty shed without spoiling.
2. **The "First Five Minutes" Farmer Experience:** The farmer places crop bags on a clean digital platform scale, hears the beep, watches the exact LED weight, receives an instant SMS/printed receipt, and gets a bank transfer ding on his phone. This single magical experience destroys 50 years of middleman mistrust.
3. **Craft in Unseen Places:** Moisture testing with calibrated digital meters, standardized 50kg branded HDPE bags with tamper-proof stitching, and transparent weight tickets.

## What Would Kill It (Top 3 Risks)
1. **Operational Clutter:** Trying to handle 10 different crops at once. Start with ONLY two (e.g., Groundnut and Toor Dal) before adding millets or cotton.
2. **Weighing & Grading Disputes:** If a farmer feels ONSFC's moisture meter is cheating him, the magical trust is instantly broken and village word-of-mouth turns hostile.
3. **Logistics Friction:** Delays in sending the pickup vehicle to the village, leaving harvested crop exposed to rain or pest attack.

## The Questions for the Founder
- What are the ONLY TWO crops ONSFC will trade in its first 90 days?
- How will you train the village collection team to handle grading disputes transparently in front of the farmer?

## Conditions for Funding
1. Launch with strict crop discipline: Focus 100% of energy on **Groundnut (Peanut)** and **Red Gram (Toor Dal)** for the first quarter.
2. Zero ambiguity at intake: Keep a physical demonstration sieve and certified calibration weight visible in the Gooty shed so any farmer can test the scale himself.
