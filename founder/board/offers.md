# The Offers Lens (Alex Hormozi Framework)
**Score: 8.5 / 10 | Vote: FUND WITH CONDITIONS**

## What is Strong
1. **Massive Market Pain:** Small farmers in Gooty lose 6-10% of their crop value to unscrupulous middlemen through doctored mechanical weighing scales and arbitrary "dalali" commissions.
2. **High-Value B2B Buyers with Budget:** Oil mills, dal mills, and large residential colleges (Narayana, Sri Chaitanya) buy dozens of tonnes monthly and have steady, predictable cash flows.
3. **Dream Outcome:** For the farmer: instant, guaranteed payment at the farm gate with 0% commission. For the buyer: verified moisture-tested Grade-A produce delivered on schedule with zero adulteration.

## What Would Kill It (Top 3 Risks)
1. **Working Capital Choke:** If institutional buyers (hostels, mills) demand 30-day payment credit while farmers require instant 24-hour bank transfer, ONSFC will run out of cash within 3 weeks of scaling unless a rolling invoice factoring or bank overdraft facility is secured.
2. **Competing Solely on Price:** If ONSFC only offers "we are cheaper", competitors and big traders will temporarily undercut ONSFC to starve it of volume. ONSFC must compete on **speed, weighing integrity, and convenience**.
3. **Quality / Moisture Dispute at Buyer End:** If Gooty groundnuts or toor dal are wet (>9% moisture) or contain soil, the oil mill or dal mill will reject the entire 10-tonne truck, creating catastrophic cash loss.

## The Questions for the Founder
- How will you bridge the 15-to-30 day buyer credit window while paying farmers in 24 hours?
- What moisture testing protocol will you enforce at the farm gate before loading onto the truck?

## Conditions for Funding
1. Mandate digital moisture testing (<8.5%) and automated digital scale receipts at the farm gate before paying.
2. Enforce 50% advance or max 7-day payment terms with institutional buyers; do not give 30-day credit without bank letter of credit.
